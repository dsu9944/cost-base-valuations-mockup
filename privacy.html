What this site does today

We don’t collect property documents on this website. There is no evidence vault, no ID upload, and no private-doc intake on the public site. The free desktop estimate uses address + tax date only in the browser (illustrative figures until the model is live).

If you request a signed valuation, the enquire form collects name, email, optional phone, property address, tax date, and message and sends them via Formspree (or equivalent) so we can follow up. Paperwork, if needed, is requested securely after we speak — not uploaded here.

This site does not run a live estimate engine. Free results are indicative only and not for tax. Signed valuation services are for NSW & VIC residential only — not Australia-wide.

Embedded videos

Some pages show news videos using YouTube's privacy-enhanced player. Before you press play, your browser loads a preview image from YouTube (owned by Google), which receives your IP address and basic browser details. If you press play, YouTube may set cookies and collect information about your viewing under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We don't control that collection and we don't receive personal information about you from YouTube.
