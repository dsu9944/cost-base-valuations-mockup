FAQ

Quick answers

Is this an online estimate or AVM?

No. We sell AVI-signed residential market valuation reports. Not an AVM, automated valuation, instant estimate, or AI valuation. Any modelling stays back-office — it isn’t the product.

Should I try records before ordering?

Yes when you can. Title, stamp duty, and an old contract often close a cost-base gap for less money. If those fail (or you need a specific valuation date), start a signed report request.

Who signs the report?

A suitably qualified AVI valuer (George) prepares and signs completed reports. That’s the accountability on the document.

What’s the difference between the two reports?

CGT cost base ($229) is a signed desktop retrospective as at a past date you nominate — common when purchase or improvement records are incomplete. 30 June 2027 ($369) is an inspected market valuation as at that fixed date.

How fast will I get it?

Desktop CGT retrospective: 3 business days. Inspected 30 June 2027: 5–7 business days. Regional or complex jobs may take longer and may quote from $449.

Can my accountant use it?

Yes — that’s the point. Pass the signed market valuation to your adviser for cost-base and CGT workflows. We’re not giving tax advice ourselves.
