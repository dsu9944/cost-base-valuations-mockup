FAQ

Short answers

I’m not selling — why now?

The tax date that splits a later gain can arrive before any sale. For many investors that’s 30 June 2027. Locking value closer to the day is clearer than reconstructing years later. Not tax advice.

Isn’t the ATO formula free?

Yes — and free can still mis-split the gain. A full market valuation by a qualified valuer as at the tax date is the comparison. Our free step is only an indicative estimate — not for tax.

How is this different from Domain or REA?

They usually estimate “today.” We frame value as at a CGT date for NSW & VIC residential. Free = indicative. Paid = signed by a qualified valuer your accountant can consider for tax records. The ATO (or others) may not accept or may disagree with a valuation. Not tax advice.

What if I moved out and rented?

Often the first-rented date. A signed retrospective as at that day is what many accountants ask for.
