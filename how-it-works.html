How Cost Base Valuations works

A simple funnel for investors and their advisers: recover documents where possible, then instruct a signed desktop report only when you need one.

Records first

Attempt title, stamp duty / duty assessment, and old contract recovery before paying for a valuation.

Submit intake

Property, purpose, date, client details, docs on hand, and records status — captured as a handoff JSON.

Valuer signs

George (licensed valuer) reviews the file and signs the desktop report. No unsupervised AVM as the final figure.

Why signing matters

📄

Adviser-ready

AU

Australian residential focus

Scope is D2C residential investors in Australia — clear property types and state/postcode intake.

What “desktop” means here:

A desktop valuation is prepared from available information without a physical inspection, unless a future product variant says otherwise. Scope and limitations are stated on the signed report.
