Step one · cheaper path

Try to recover title, stamp duty, and old contracts first

Many cost-base gaps close with documents you or your conveyancer can still obtain. We lead with this step so you only instruct a signed valuation when records aren’t enough.

T

Title / ownership history

Title search and historical ownership can confirm transfer dates and parties — useful anchors for cost base timelines.

S

Stamp duty / duty assessment

Duty notices or assessments often record consideration or dutiable value around a transfer — check state revenue records where available.

C

Old contract of sale

Purchase contracts, settlement statements, and related solicitor files are the primary source when they still exist.

Possible outcomes

Records recovered

You (and your accountant) may have enough to reconstruct cost base without a valuation. Keep copies organised; no signed report required from us unless you still want one for a specific date.

Next: Share documents with your tax adviser.

Records failed / incomplete

Gaps remain for the date or component you need. Continue to a signed desktop valuation — retrospective for CGT, or market value as at 30 June 2027.
